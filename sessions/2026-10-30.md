## Open questions

1. **Eligibility.** Any federal recognition statute has to define which entities qualify. That is a design question for the paper body, not the thesis.
2. **Lido and liability.** Does the remedy preserve liability for actors who actually control an entity? Lido turns on governance participation, so the paper needs an answer.
3. **Insurers.** The claim about insurers needs a source in Phase 3, or it should be cut.
4. **Submission cycles.** Confirm fall 2027 and spring 2027 submission windows and word limits for the target blockchain and technology journals [VERIFY].
5. **Statutory landscape** [VERIFY]: the Wyoming DAO LLC (2021) and DUNA (2024), the Utah DAO Act (2024), any IRS guidance on DAO classification, and federal market-structure legislation as of Oct. 2026.
6. **Resolved: the bind's wording** (decisions 17–18).
7. **Scope of the Swiss claim.** The supervisory authority checks legality; it does not vote on protocol upgrades. The claim has to name what the foundation actually controls (treasury, IP and trademarks, grants, contracts) and where the supervisor has the final say (organization, purpose, dissolution, intervention in council deficiencies) [VERIFY].
8. **Token holders.** Can a foundation deed or regulations give token holders a binding role, given that a Swiss foundation has no members [VERIFY]? If they can, the claim needs a carve-out.
9. **Sampling frame for Phase 3.** The RevenueBase list is a sales lead-generation product ranked by contacts tracked. Its visible top 25 omits the Ethereum Foundation, the Web3 Foundation, the Cardano Foundation, the Interchain Foundation, and the Dfinity Foundation, so it cannot serve as the sample. Proposal: pre-register a reproducible rule before research begins, for example the top N networks by market capitalization on a fixed snapshot date whose primary steward is a Swiss foundation, confirmed against the Swiss commercial register (Zefix) and the foundation's supervisory authority [VERIFY registration requirement].
