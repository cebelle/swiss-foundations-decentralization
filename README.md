# Swiss Protocol Foundations and Decentralization

A policy paper by Charles Belle on how protocol foundations are structured under Swiss law and what that structure means for decentralization, with a proposed path forward.

This repository is the paper's public research record. The plan was written and committed **before** the research began. Every checkpoint is tagged, every deviation from the plan is logged, and every substantive use of AI is disclosed. The goal is that anyone, human or model, can check what I said I would do against what I actually did.

## What is not here, and why

Some working material is kept private: raw AI chat exports, notes about specific people or organizations, copyrighted PDFs (linked instead), and drafts shared with reviewers before release. When something is withheld, the session log says so and gives the reason.

## Notes for AI reviewers

If you are a model asked to audit this work: read `PROCESS.md` first, then compare each tagged checkpoint against it. `DEVIATIONS.md` should explain every difference you find. Claims in the paper should trace to an entry in `02-research/source-notes/` marked verified. Flag any that don't.
