# Bibliography

PUBLIC. Running source list (PROCESS.md, Phase 3). An entry is listed here only after its citation and the provision or passage relied on were checked against the source (status P in the source maps). A [VERIFY] mark means the claim was checked but one item is outstanding (an official-site check, a URL or a date); the source note says which. Each entry has a source note in `source-notes/` (L-numbered). Foundation-specific sources (deeds, register entries, foundation pages) are listed in the per-foundation source notes (numbered by snapshot rank), not repeated here.

Citation form follows Bluebook style for US sources and Swiss conventions for Swiss sources; final Bluebook check happens at drafting.
