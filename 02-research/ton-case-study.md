# TON case study: scope and research questions

**Label:** PUBLIC · **Date:** 2026-11-15 · **Status:** research in progress (Q1, Q2, Q5–Q7 facts gathered 2026-11-15; Q3–Q4 legal note drafted 2026-11-15 and held privately, see below); no argument yet · **Deviation:** logged 2026-11-15 in `DEVIATIONS.md`

## Why TON

TON tests the research question directly: what does it mean to be decentralized? The network held itself out as decentralized at every stage. Yet in 2026, operating control moved from a Swiss Stiftung under federal supervision to a for-profit company. The case lets us ask how control moved, who could authorize or block the move, and what part (if any) the Swiss supervisor played.

The thesis does not depend on where control sits. A supervisor's final veto and a company's operating control are both centralized. TON matters because it shows the mechanics of a move, and it may show the limits of what the supervisor's veto actually reaches.

## Held out as decentralized (practice layer)

- **2020, Telegram:** its public notice said TON "will be decentralized and maintained by third parties" and that Telegram would have no control over it. https://telegram.org/blog/ton-gram-notice
- **2024, TON Foundation:** launched Society DAO, framed as a step on TON's path to decentralization. https://cointelegraph.com/news/ton-foundation-new-governance-model-society-dao
- **2025, TON Foundation spokesperson:** "TON is an open, decentralized blockchain." https://regional-front.cointelegraph.com/news/telegram-ton-only-strategy-mini-apps-backfire
- **Accessed 2026-11-15, ton.foundation:** the Foundation "empowers TON projects without controlling TON technology"; TON "has no single controlling authority." https://ton.foundation/en
- **May 2026, Durov:** said Telegram becoming the largest validator "strengthens decentralization." https://thedefiant.io/toncoin-surges-as-telegram-takes-direct-control-of-ton-network

## Research questions

1. **Assets.** Which Foundation assets moved to Telegram or Telegram-linked entities: the ton.org domain (reported as Foundation-owned in 2023 [VERIFY]), GitHub organisations, trademarks (US application 79435217 in the Foundation's name; verified 2026-11-15, see Q1), treasury or Toncoin holdings? On what terms?
2. **Authority.** Who decided? Did the Stiftung council pass a resolution? Did the deed or any regulations (*Reglemente*) need to change? (The deed has not been amended since 2023 per the Zug register.)
3. **Supervisor.** Did the 2026 change require EDI notice or approval, and did EDI act? The provisions to test are Art. 84 para. 2 ZGB (use of assets), Art. 83d ZGB (organisational defects), Arts. 85–86c ZGB (changes to the deed), Arts. 88–89 ZGB (dissolution) and Arts. 86–87 FusG (transfers of the estate). [VERIFY practice] This is the direct test of the thesis claim about the final veto. *Corrected 2026-11-15: this question first cited "Arts. 84 and 84a ZGB". Art. 84a deals with over-indebtedness and insolvency and does not govern transfers.*
4. **Purpose.** Is a Stiftung whose network is now run by a company still pursuing its registered purpose? What could the supervisor do about it (Art. 84 ZGB supervision; Art. 86a purpose change; Art. 88 dissolution)? [VERIFY]
5. **Token holders and validators.** Did on-chain governance (validator config votes) ratify any of the changes? The April 2026 fee cut went to a validator vote.
6. **ADGM entity.** What did the TON DLT Foundation hold or do, and did anything move through it?
7. **Disclosure.** What did exchanges and the Foundation tell token holders about the change?

## Findings, 2026-11-15 (session 2: Opus 5.5, High). Facts only, no argument

The three layers are kept apart. **Law** is the deed, the register and statutes. **Practice** is what the entities did and said. The **author's argument** does not appear here.

### Q1. Assets

- **Trademark (verified in TSDR, 2026-11-15).** US 79435217 is a Madrid extension (IR 1882072, dated 22.05.2025, priority 20.12.2024). It covers the TON logo as a design mark: a circle containing an inverted triangle with a vertical line. The owner is "The Open Network Foundation", c/o Sielva Management AG, Gubelstrasse 11, Zug. Status: live, with a non-final Office action (partial refusal) on 18.08.2026. The Foundation's counsel filed a response on 05.06.2026, a month after the takeover announcement. The status record shows no assignment. **So the logo mark had not moved as of today.**
- **The token's new name.** The token is now "Gram", and on 18.08.2026 Telegram, not the Foundation, applied to ICANN for .gram. Whether anyone holds a US or Swiss GRAM mark: [VERIFY TSDR and Swissreg].
- **ton.org.** RDAP shows a registrar transfer on 30.03.2026 (to GoDaddy). That is ten days before MTONGA step 1 and five weeks before the 4 May announcement. The registrant is hidden behind Domains By Proxy, so **we cannot tell from public data who holds the domain, or whether the holder changed.** A registrar transfer does not by itself mean a change of owner. The 2021 handover went to the "TON Foundation" when it was still an unincorporated community. The Stiftung was formed in 2023. Whether the Stiftung ever held the domain is [VERIFY]. If it never did, the domain was never a Stiftung asset that could "move".
- **Code.** ton.org links to github.com/ton-blockchain. Telegram's new toolchain (Acton, 11.05.2026) is hosted under that org (ton-blockchain.github.io/acton). Who controls the org could not be checked from here [VERIFY]. The "TON Core" team ships the node software. Telegram co-ran core contests with TON Core from January 2025, sixteen months before the announcement. Who employs TON Core: [VERIFY].
- **Treasury.** Still undisclosed. ton.foundation says the Foundation is "funded entirely by community contributions". Secondary sources report Telegram's validator stake at about 2.2M Gram. In 2025 Coinbase Ventures bought Toncoin "directly from Telegram", per the Foundation's then-president, so Telegram holds the token. TON Strategy Co. held about 221.9M at 31.03.2026, and its own figures put about 26% of network stake through its infrastructure. No source shows Stiftung-held tokens moving to Telegram.

### Q5. Validators and token holders

- **Telegram becoming a validator needed no vote.** Staking is open to anyone who meets the elector's terms [VERIFY elector parameters].
- **On-chain votes in the period:** the Catchain 2.0 activation (votes 2–8 April 2026; needs at least 75% of validators by stake over two rounds, per t.me/gram/2368) and the gas-price config vote (30.04.2026).
