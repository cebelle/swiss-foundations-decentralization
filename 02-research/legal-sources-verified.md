## Art. 86a ZGB (founder's reservation)

The official text confirms the codebook's summary (F3):

- The competent federal or cantonal authority changes the purpose or the organisation at the founder's request (para. 1). The founder does not make the change.
- The deed must reserve the change, and at least ten years must have passed "seit der Errichtung der Stiftung" or since the last change the founder requested (para. 1). The statute adds that the periods run independently ("Die Fristen laufen unabhängig voneinander", para. 1). The codebook's line "The two periods run separately" paraphrases this sentence. Which periods it refers to is a question of interpretation, not checked here.
- A changed purpose must stay public or charitable if the foundation is tax-exempt as such (para. 2).
- The right cannot be inherited or transferred. If the founder is a legal person, it ends no later than 20 years after the foundation's establishment (para. 3).
- Several founders can only request a change jointly (para. 4).
- The amended wording of paras. 1, 3 and 4 is in force since 1 January 2024 (Federal Act of 17 December 2021, AS 2022 452).

**Bears on a question held for Fable:** the statute counts the ten years from the foundation's establishment ("Errichtung"). Under Art. 52 para. 1 ZGB a foundation acquires legal personality on entry in the commercial register. Whether "Errichtung" means the deed date, the register entry or the SHAB publication is a Swiss-law question. It stays [VERIFY] and is held for Fable, as the author decided on 2026-11-17. It can move dates in F3 but not values.

## Transitional provision

No transitional provision for the Federal Act of 17 December 2021 was found in the final title of the ZGB on Fedlex (full-text search for the Act's title, 2026-11-18). This is consistent with the codebook's note that the statute has no transitional rule for pre-2024 deeds. The University of Zurich Foundation Law Day materials (2023) cited for the debate on pre-2024 deeds have not been re-checked. They stay [VERIFY] until added to the bibliography with a source note.
